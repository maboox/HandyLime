## What's new

- **Adjust video:** the photo editor's 14 light, color and detail controls now work with videos. Review a cached frame, compare with the original, scrub the timeline or play a short edited sample before saving the whole video as a new MP4.
- **Start with Windows:** an optional setting starts HandyLime minimized in the tray after you sign in. It is off by default and can be removed or updated from Settings.
- **Preview navigation:** rendered samples have a Back to editor button and visible playback errors. The sidebar now displays the actual app version.

Video editing requires FFmpeg and FFprobe. Both Portable and Lite include the same features. This remains a preview release.
